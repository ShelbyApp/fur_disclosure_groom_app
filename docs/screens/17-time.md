# A little more time?

**Route:** `#/time` · **Stage:** What-ifs · **Design reference:** 19

## Purpose

An extra-time allowance does not approve extra fees or a shorter cut.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **If more working time is needed…** — radio · required: Ask me first / Up to 15 extra minutes / Up to 30 extra minutes

## Navigation

Selectable in [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) or individually from review. Save returns to the originating screen.

## Behavior and acceptance

An explicit time choice is required; selecting minutes grants no permission for fees or a style change.
