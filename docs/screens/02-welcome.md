# A little prep. A happier groom.

**Route:** `#/welcome` · **Stage:** Role / visit handoff · **Design reference:** 01

## Purpose

Choose how you’d like to plan this visit.

## Inputs and displayed content

Basic groom and Advanced groom cards. An existing visit also shows resume or view, and an action to start a different visit.

## Navigation

Both new modes lead to dog details. Resume restores the current visit.

## Behavior and acceptance

Choose Basic or Advanced before entering dog details. Basic covers the service, care and contingencies. Advanced also offers uploads, styling and notes placed on photos. Switching modes keeps shared answers. A different visit requires an explicit reset.
