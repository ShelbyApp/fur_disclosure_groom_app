# Your pup, as they are today.

**Route:** `#/current-photos` · **Stage:** Your dog · **Design reference:** 03

## Purpose

Show the coat your groomer will see at drop-off. All photos are optional.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **Face today** — photo · optional
- **Side today** — photo · optional
- **Back today** — photo · optional

## Navigation

advanced haircut path: [dog](03-dog.md) → [current-photos](04-current-photos.md) → [service](05-service.md).

## Behavior and acceptance

Advanced only. Face, side and back are optional independent photo slots. Tap a photo to add a numbered note. Enlarge, edit notes, replace or remove the photo. Replacing a marked photo clears its notes after confirmation.

Photo controls accept JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 12 MB. Resize locally, keep a thumbnail and report a readable error without losing the draft.
