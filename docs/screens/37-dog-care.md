# Usual care and what-ifs

**Route** `#/dog-care` with dog ID

Separate next page after dog basics. Set nervous level and independent defaults for matting, extra time, fees, distress and missed contact. Empty choices mean Choose at each visit. The Health & comfort section collects optional allergies, medications or relevant conditions, mobility and comfort notes. Health text is saved alongside other defaults; cleared values are removed. Saving preserves weight and any unrelated defaults, then returns to the dog profile. A link edits basic details. Changes prefill future fresh visits, not historical plans or active drafts.
