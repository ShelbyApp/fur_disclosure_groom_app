# Let’s keep in touch.

**Route:** `#/contact` · **Stage:** What-ifs · **Design reference:** 22

## Purpose

Contact details are optional. Choose whether to include them in your finished plan.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **Your name** — text · optional
- **Phone number** — tel · optional
- **Email address** — email · optional
- **Secondary contact name** — text · optional
- **Secondary contact phone** — tel · optional
- **Preferred contact method** — radio · optional: Call / Text / Email
- **Include these contact details in the groomer’s copy** — check · optional

## Behavior and acceptance

Owner name, sign-in email and saved phone appear in a read-only card. Add secondary contact expands optional name and phone fields for this visit. There is no Edit contact for this visit control; primary details are maintained on the owner profile. A copied groom can retain its secondary contact, but contact sharing must be chosen again. An unchecked inclusion choice removes primary contact, secondary contact and preferred channel from the finished sheet. An entered email in a legacy guest draft is validated.
