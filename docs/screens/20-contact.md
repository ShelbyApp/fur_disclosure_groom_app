# Let’s keep in touch.

**Route:** `#/contact` · **Stage:** What-ifs · **Design reference:** 22

## Purpose

Contact details are optional. Choose whether to include them in your finished plan.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **Your name** — text · optional
- **Phone number** — tel · optional
- **Email address** — email · optional
- **Preferred contact method** — radio · optional: Call / Text / Email
- **Include these contact details in the groomer’s copy** — check · optional

## Navigation

basic haircut path: [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) → [contact](20-contact.md) → [review-look](22-review-look.md). advanced haircut path: [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) → [contact](20-contact.md) → [review-look](22-review-look.md).

## Behavior and acceptance

Owner name, sign-in email and saved phone fill in the contact screen. Visit edits do not change the profile. Guests can enter contact details here. An unchecked inclusion choice removes all contact fields, including the preferred channel, from the finished sheet. An entered email is validated.
