# Care comes first.

**Route:** `#/what-ifs` · **Stage:** What-ifs · **Design reference:** 17

## Purpose

Set your what-ifs before the visit.

## Inputs and displayed content

Five expandable rows, each with a separate required choice. A sensitive-spot card opens the guide map.

## Navigation

basic haircut path: [handling](13-handling.md) → [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) → [contact](20-contact.md). advanced haircut path: [handling](13-handling.md) → [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) → [contact](20-contact.md).

## Behavior and acceptance

Five expandable rows show separate choices for matting, time, fees, distress and missed contact. Tap a choice to save and fold the row. Continue opens the first unfinished row. The guide map remains available here in both modes.
