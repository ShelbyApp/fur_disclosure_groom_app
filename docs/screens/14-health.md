# What keeps your pup comfortable?

**Route:** `#/health` · **Stage:** Care needs · **Design reference:** 16

## Purpose

Share care information that matters for this visit. “None known” and “Not sure” are useful answers.

- **Allergies or product sensitivities** — text · optional
- **Medications or relevant conditions** — text · optional
- **Mobility or standing concerns** — text · optional
- **Anything else for their comfort?** — note · optional
