# Household profile

**Route** `#/profile`

Show the saved owner, a Your dogs section, and a Your groomers section. Each dog opens its profile; each groomer opens their editable contact. Add multiple dogs or groomers, edit owner information or sign out. Groomers remain private owner-entered contacts; there is no public directory lookup. Signing out clears account-bound drafts on this tab.
