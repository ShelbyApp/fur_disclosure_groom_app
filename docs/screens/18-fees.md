# No surprise extras.

**Route:** `#/fees` · **Stage:** What-ifs · **Design reference:** 20

## Purpose

This is your limit, not a quote. It doesn’t approve a different cut or extra time.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **If there’s an extra fee…** — radio · required: Ask before any added fee / Up to $15 extra / Up to $30 extra

## Navigation

Selectable in [what-ifs](15-what-ifs.md) or individually from review. Save returns to the originating screen.

## Behavior and acceptance

Choose one limit explicitly. Examples are owner allowances, not salon quotes or payments. No time or cut permission is inferred.
