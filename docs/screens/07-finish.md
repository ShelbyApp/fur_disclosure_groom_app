# A little this. A little that.

**Route:** `#/finish` · **Stage:** The groom · **Design reference:** 07

## Purpose

Describe the finish you have in mind. Your groomer will check what’s possible.

## Inputs and displayed content

- **Overall finish** — range · optional: Short & tidy / A little shorter / Balanced / Some extra fluff / Long & fluffy
- **Styling priority** — radio · optional: Simple & practical / More detail, if comfortable / Discuss together

## Navigation

advanced haircut path: [inspiration](06-inspiration.md) → [finish](07-finish.md) → [face](08-face.md). Advanced only. Bath-only skips this chapter. Remaining look details can be skipped.

## Behavior and acceptance

A five-position slider and named tap choices control the same value. No value is saved until a choice is made. It is not an exact remaining length.
