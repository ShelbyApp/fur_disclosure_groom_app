# Find a groomer

**Route** `#/find-groomer/new` or `#/find-groomer/new/visit`

Enter and select a city, then see 20 businesses at a time and refine by name, service and salon/mobile location. Mobile providers can serve several cities through one record. Cards show confirmed services, location, available rating and contact details. Selection saves an owner contact and returns to the matching active groom draft when entered from a visit. Add a missing groomer opens the manual form with the chosen city. Loading, empty, unsupported-city and retry states keep the manual-entry path available.
