# Find a groomer

**Route** `#/find-groomer/new` or `#/find-groomer/new/visit`

Enter and select a city to center a styled Google map on that area. City suggestions and business cards appear automatically only when there are four or fewer matches. The map stays above Search business name; selecting a salon pin previews its card below the search. Typing clears that selection immediately. See all results overrides the four-result limit for the current filters, with Show more results appending 20 at a time. Mobile providers are found by their supported city coverage, without inventing exact pins or coverage boundaries.

Cards show confirmed services, location, available rating and contact details. Selection saves an owner contact and returns to the matching active groom draft when entered from a visit. Add a missing groomer opens the manual form with the chosen city. Map failures, loading, empty, unsupported-city and retry states keep search and manual entry available.
