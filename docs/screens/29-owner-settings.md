# Owner details

**Route** `#/owner-settings`

Show the account email and save the required owner name and optional phone, city and state. First-time Meet the owner has no password-change or sign-out controls. On first save, open dog basics. Later edits return to the household; an established profile can change its password here. Sign out lives on the household screen.
